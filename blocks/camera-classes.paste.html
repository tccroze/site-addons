Camera Classes

Most of what I know about a camera I learned by getting it wrong on somebody else’s time. These classes are the shorter way round: small groups, your own camera, and a day given over to the handful of decisions that actually change a picture. Two tracks, stills and motion, taught the same way and in whichever order suits you.

Track One

Photography

Light, exposure, and the edge of the frame. Two classes: start at the beginning, or arrive with a few thousand frames behind you and fix what keeps going wrong.
