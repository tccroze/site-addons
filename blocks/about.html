Guiding

In the field

Five years guiding safaris and managing luxury lodges taught me patience, timing, and how to catch the moment before it’s gone. I learned to notice the things most people walk past: light, colour, movement, the small details that tell the bigger story.

That’s what I bring to your project. Nothing rushed, nothing missed.
